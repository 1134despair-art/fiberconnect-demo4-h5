2. Products and Services

Depending on the client type, account permissions, device model, and network conditions, the current version may provide some or all of the following features:

  • Registration and sign-in using a mobile number, email address, verification code, or password; quick sign-in to the WeChat Mini Program using WeChat sign-in and mobile-number authorization;

  • Browsing product categories, product information, About information, and operating manuals;
  • Scanning for, connecting, binding, unbinding, and managing compatible Bluetooth devices;
  • Scanning a device QR code, connecting through a 4G channel, and issuing control instructions;
  • Device activation, authorization-period settings, device startup-password settings, firmware checks, and updates;
  • Operating optical power meters (OPM), visual fault locators (VFL), OTDR devices, fusion splicers, and other compatible devices, and viewing, saving, deleting, or exporting relevant records;
  • Submitting and checking after-sales orders, logistics information, and processing results; and
  • Switching the interface or device language and using sharing capabilities provided by the WeChat platform.

Features may vary by platform. For example, the App may support registration and sign-in using a mobile number or email address. The WeChat Mini Program uses a WeChat temporary login credential and mobile-number authorization; the server identifies, creates, or links an account based on the authorized mobile number, subject to server verification. The current Mini Program provides no alternative sign-in method if mobile-number authorization is refused. Camera, Bluetooth, photo-album, and file capabilities are also subject to operating-system or WeChat platform restrictions. The displayed page, actual device capabilities, and server results prevail.

The current version does not provide user-facing payment, advertising, public-content publishing, or social-relationship features. If such features are added later, we will update the applicable rules and obtain separate consent where required by law.

2. You confirm that you have lawful ownership, management rights, or authorization to use every device you connect, bind, activate, or control. Do not connect devices from unknown sources, bypass access controls, or use the services to control a device you are not authorized to operate.

3. Bluetooth, 4G, network conditions, device firmware, system version, battery level, distance, signal strength, and hardware status may affect connections and instruction execution. A page message stating "Sent" or "Connected" does not guarantee that the device has completed the intended physical action in every case. You must verify the device display, indicators, on-site conditions, and operating instructions.

4. Device activation codes, administrator passwords, startup passwords, and authorization information are security credentials. You must safeguard them and not disclose them to unrelated persons. Liability for unauthorized device access caused by disclosure or misuse will be borne by the party at fault in accordance with law.

5. Fusion splicing, heating, red-light output, OTDR testing, firmware upgrades, factory resets, calibration, and other device operations require professional competence. Follow device instructions and on-site safety rules, and confirm the safety of the device, personnel, and optical path before operating. Recheck the target before performing an operation with irreversible consequences.
