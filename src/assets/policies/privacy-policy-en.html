Huizhou Senkelin Optoelectronic Technology Co., Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") values your personal information and privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information when providing FiberConnect services, and how you may exercise your rights.

1. Scope and Basic Principles

1. This Policy applies to the FiberConnect App, the FiberConnect WeChat Mini Program, and the account, product, device, record, after-sales, operating-manual, and firmware services accessed through those clients.

2. We follow the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy, necessity, good faith, and data minimization. We process personal information only to the extent required to provide clearly defined features or fulfill legal obligations.

3. Third-party websites, WeChat platform capabilities, and operating-system services are independently provided by their respective third parties and are governed by their own personal-information rules. This Policy identifies the principal third-party services directly related to this product.

2. When We Obtain Your Consent

1. The mobile-number/email registration and sign-in pages in the App, and the quick sign-in page using a WeChat mobile number in the Mini Program, provide links to the User Agreement and this Policy. The application submits the relevant sign-in, registration, or mobile-number authorization request only after you select the consent option.

2. Bluetooth, camera, photo-album, and similar device permissions are requested by the application or operating system when you use the corresponding feature. The WeChat Mini Program may also display privacy notices in accordance with WeChat platform rules.

3. To load splash images, public product information, operating manuals, agreement pages, About information, and basic application functions, the application may send network requests that do not contain sign-in credentials before you sign in.

4. The current App and WeChat Mini Program builds have DCloud uni Statistics 2.0 enabled. From application startup, this component may automatically process basic device information, application operation data, page-visit data, and network-environment information before you submit consent for sign-in or registration. The current version does not provide a separate switch to disable this analytics component. If you do not agree to this continued processing, please close and stop using the application or contact us using the methods listed in this Policy.

6. Local Storage, Cookies, and Logs

1. The application stores sign-in tokens, cached account-profile data, cached bound-device data, WeChat Mini Program Bluetooth identifier mappings, device control channels, cached operating manuals, and necessary runtime state locally on the device. Signing out clears the sign-in token and cached account-profile data, but other feature caches may remain. You can clear them by using the system's Clear App Data function, deleting the Mini Program, or uninstalling the application.

2. The H5 version may use browser local storage or similar technologies to maintain functionality. This product does not use cookies or similar technologies to track your activity across unrelated websites or applications.

3. To diagnose problems, the application may record request addresses and results, device communication status, QR-code connection results, errors, and operation times in local runtime logs. Do not voluntarily include passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive information unrelated to troubleshooting in a support request.

7. Storage Location, Retention, and Cross-Border Transfers

1. In principle, personal information processed by our business servers is stored within the People's Republic of China. Current business APIs use HTTPS for transmission.

2. We retain personal information for the shortest period required for the purposes described in this Policy, considering account duration, device and after-sales needs, dispute resolution, security audits, and legal requirements. Actual retention periods vary by data type. Data subject to statutory retention requirements is retained for the legally required period and then deleted or anonymized.

3. Local caches can be cleared by clearing application data, deleting the Mini Program, or uninstalling the application. Only some server-side OPM, VFL, and similar records currently support individual deletion or clearing within the application. For other server records, submit a request through the contact methods in this Policy.

4. Our current business does not transfer personal information abroad as a business purpose. If a cross-border transfer occurs in the future, we will provide notice, obtain separate consent, and complete other procedures required by law.

8. How We Protect Information

We use access controls, identity authentication, HTTPS transmission, permission management, and security audits appropriate to our business risks. Internet and wireless communications cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please safeguard your account, password, verification codes, device startup password, and activation information, and do not disclose them to unrelated persons.

If a personal-information security incident may affect your rights and interests, we will take remedial measures and notify you through in-app notices, available contact details, or another reasonable method as required by law.

9. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You have the legal right to access, copy, correct, supplement, and delete personal information, withdraw consent, request an explanation of processing rules, and cancel your account.

  • Access and correction: The current application allows you to view account details and change your nickname and email address on the Settings page. To request changes to your mobile number or other information, contact us using the methods below.
  • Record management: Some OPM, VFL, and similar records support individual deletion or clearing. For other server records, contact us using the methods below.
  • Sign out: The Settings page and Personal Center provide a sign-out feature. Signing out only clears the local sign-in state and does not cancel your account.
  • Account cancellation: The current version does not provide an in-app self-service account-cancellation entry. You may submit a cancellation request through our customer-service number, privacy email address, or postal address. After verifying your identity and resolving device bindings and incomplete after-sales matters, we will generally cancel the account within 15 business days and delete or anonymize account details, account-device relationships, and server-side business records that are no longer required, except where retention is required by law. You must clear local device caches yourself.
  • Withdrawal of permissions: You can disable Bluetooth, camera, photo-album, and similar permissions using the system paths listed in Section 4.

To protect account security, we may require identity-verification information appropriate to your request. We will generally respond within 15 business days after verifying your identity, unless otherwise required by law.

10. Protection of Minors

This product is primarily intended for adults with the ability to manage and professionally operate fiber-optic equipment. Users under 18 should read this Policy under a guardian's guidance and obtain the guardian's consent before use. Children under 14 must be registered, authorized, and supervised by a guardian. We do not process minors' personal information for the purpose of marketing to minors.
